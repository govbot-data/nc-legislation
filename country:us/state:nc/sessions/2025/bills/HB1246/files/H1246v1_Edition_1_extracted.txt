8 SECTION 1. Article 22 of Chapter 163 of the General Statutes is amended by adding
9 a new section to read:
10 "§ 163-271.2. Interference with conduct of elections.
11 (a) A person acting under the color of law shall not order or authorize the presence of any
12 troops or armed persons in the civil, military, or naval service of this State or the United States
13 at any location used as a voting place, including any parking areas used for voting, beginning 17
14 days before an election through the close of the voting place on election day, unless such force
15 is necessary to repel armed enemies of the United States. For purposes of this section, "color of
16 law" means to act or purport to act in the performance of official duties under the authority of a
17 statute, ordinance, regulation, rule, proclamation, or order of the United States or this State.
18 (b) Nothing in this section shall be construed to prevent any officer or member of any
19 troops or the Armed Forces of this State or the United States from exercising the right to vote.
20 (c) This section shall not apply to (i) law enforcement whose presence is otherwise
21 required by law or who is responding to a request for assistance regarding a specific emergency
22 or disturbance that exists at the time the request for assistance is made or (ii) a request for
23 assistance from an election officer with jurisdiction over the election process in question.
24 (d) Any person aggrieved by a violation of this section may bring an action for preventive
25 relief, including an application in a district court for a permanent or temporary injunction,
26 restraining order, or other order. In any action commenced pursuant to this section, the court, in
27 its discretion, may allow the prevailing party reasonable attorneys' fees.
28 (e) In addition to any other fine or penalty imposed by this section, the court may order
29 any person liable for violating this section to pay a civil penalty, the amount of which shall be
30 determined by the court and be commensurate with the seriousness of the offense.
31 (f) This section shall be enforced in addition to any other existing civil and criminal
32 penalties established under this Chapter."

51 politics.

22 PART VIII. VOTER PROTECTION AND RELIANCE ACT
23 SECTION 8.(a) Chapter 163 of the General Statutes is amended by adding a new

25 "Article 15B.
26 "Voter Protection and Reliance Act.
27 "§ 163-182.50. Title; purpose.
28 (a) This Article shall be known and may be cited as the "Voter Protection and Reliance
29 Act."
30 (b) The General Assembly finds that a voter relies on the election procedures that are in
31 place and established when the voter casts the voter's ballot. Belated changes to voting
32 procedures, after a voter has relied on those procedures while voting, violate the voter's
33 good-faith reliance and right to vote. To that end, the purpose of this Article is to ensure
34 challenges to the rules and practices governing elections are made well in advance of each
35 election by (i) prohibiting the discounting of ballots cast in compliance with the laws established
36 and understood at the time of an election and (ii) accelerating litigation filed shortly before a
37 pending election or after an election is held.
38 "§ 163-182.51. Right to vote under prevailing law and procedures.
39 (a) Every citizen of this State has the right to cast a ballot and have their vote counted
40 based on the laws and established election procedures in place on election day. No law or
41 interpretation of law that was not effective, or election procedure that was not established, on
42 election day shall operate to deny or abridge this right.
43 (b) No administrative body, court, or official may exclude a vote from canvass, fail to
44 certify an election, withhold a certificate of election, or fail to seat a prevailing candidate by
45 excluding votes based on a law, judicial ruling, or legal interpretation that was not in effect on
46 election day or an election procedure that was not established by election day.
47 (c) In any civil action filed in a State court, a protest before the State Board or a county
48 board of elections, or other proceeding, the adjudicator shall deem a law effective and an election
49 procedure established when either of the following applies:
