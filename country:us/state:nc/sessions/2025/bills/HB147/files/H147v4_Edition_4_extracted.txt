9 SECTION 2.(a) Chapter 113 of the General Statutes is amended by adding a new

11 "Article 22C.
12 "Currituck County Game Commission.
13 "§ 113-300.10. Regulation of waterfowl hunting in Currituck County.
14 The hunting, shooting, killing, or trapping of any wild fowl consisting of geese, ducks, brant,
15 or any other wild fowl from shore, marsh, blind, or floating device on or adjacent to the public
16 waters of Currituck County shall be governed and regulated by the North Carolina Wildlife
17 Resources Commission and the Game Commission of Currituck County, as set forth in this
18 Article, subject to the regulations of this Article.
19 "§ 113-300.11. Definitions.
20 The following definitions shall apply in this Article:
21 (1) Float blind. – A floating device, including a boat, operated on the public
22 waters of Currituck County from which wild fowl are hunted under a license
23 issued by the Game Commission pursuant to G.S. 113-300.16, and that
24 conforms to the rules for float blinds adopted by the Game Commission.
25 (2) Game Commission. – The Game Commission of Currituck County.
26 (3) Point blind. – A blind located entirely upon the private property of an
27 individual or organization for which a license has been issued by the Game
28 Commission in accordance with the provisions of G.S. 113-300.17.
29 (4) Stationary bush blind. – A blind located entirely within the public waters of
30 Currituck County at a location established by the Game Commission.
31 "§ 113-300.12. Hunting licenses.
32 Hunting licenses for shooting migratory wild fowl on the waters covered by this Article shall
33 conform to the licenses set out or required by the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission
34 or as required by the statewide game law.
35 "§ 113-300.13. Game Commission of Currituck County created; membership; terms;
36 vacancies; powers.
37 (a) Creation; Membership. – A Game Commission of Currituck County is created. The
38 Game Commission shall consist of seven members, each of whom shall be thoroughly acquainted
39 with migratory waterfowl shooting both ashore and afloat. The Game Commission shall be
40 selected and appointed by the Board of County Commissioners of Currituck County, but no
41 member shall be removed except upon the unanimous vote of all the members of the board of
42 commissioners. One member shall be chosen from each of the five county commissioner
43 residency districts, and two members shall be appointed to serve at large. In the event of a
44 vacancy, successors to the members of the Game Commission shall be similarly appointed.
45 (b) Terms. – Members of the Game Commission shall be appointed by the Board of
46 County Commissioners on the first Monday of June of each year as terms expire and shall hold
47 office for terms of two years, or until their successors are appointed and qualified.
48 (c) Powers. – The Game Commission, acting with the North Carolina Wildlife Resources
49 Commission, shall have charge of the enforcement of this Article and all migratory wild fowl
50 game laws in Currituck County, and the Game Commission, acting with the North Carolina
51 Wildlife Resources Commission, shall have the power and authority to prescribe rules and

20 four dollars ($4.00) of the fee for a license for a stationary bush blind, and nine dollars ($9.00)

22 "§ 113-300.15. Control over blind licenses; complaints; appeals.
23 The Game Commission shall have control over the issuance of licenses for stationary bush
24 blinds, float blinds, and point blinds, consistent with this Article and rules adopted pursuant to
25 this Article. The Game Commission shall hear complaints and petitions from individuals relative
26 to location of blinds and other matters and shall make adjustment of such matters; provided that
27 any individual not satisfied with the ruling of the Game Commission may file an appeal with the
28 district court within 10 days. The procedure governing the appeal shall substantially follow the
29 provisions of subsections (f) through (k) of G.S. 113-300.34.
30 "§ 113-300.16. Licenses for stationary bush blinds and float blinds; fees.
31 (a) To obtain a license for either a stationary bush blind or a float blind, the applicant
32 shall apply in writing to the clerk to the Game Commission, or otherwise apply by any method
33 approved by the Game Commission, including via an online application, and pay an application
34 fee and a processing fee. Application and processing fees shall be set annually by the Game
35 Commission at the budget meeting held in June, and the Game Commission may establish
36 different fees for residents of North Carolina and nonresidents.
37 (b) Applicants who are residents of North Carolina shall submit proof of North Carolina
38 residency along with each application in order to be subject to resident fees. Applicants who are
39 not residents of North Carolina but who were the holders of licensed blinds for the 1996-97
40 waterfowl season shall be charged as North Carolina residents for all subsequent renewals of that
41 application. However, this exemption terminates if the blind license is not renewed during any
42 subsequent annual renewal period and is not transferable to any different blind location. Float
43 blinds when licensed shall bear the license number, and the license number shall be displayed in
44 a prominent or conspicuous place upon the blind.
45 "§ 113-300.17. Licenses for point blinds; fees.
46 (a) Individuals owning property in Currituck County bought and used for the shooting of
47 migratory wild fowl who desire to have any shooting point or location protected by a license as
48 such shall make application in accordance with this section; provided that applications filed later
49 than August 1 shall be given consideration at subsequent meetings of the Game Commission.
50 (b) The application shall accurately describe the location, and a map shall be furnished
51 to the Game Commission upon request.

13 "§ 113-300.18. Prima facie evidence of hunting.
14 For purposes of this Article, it shall be prima facie evidence when anyone is found in a boat,
15 blind, or floating device, or on shore or marsh with decoys or geese or any other contrivance
16 which is used in hunting wild fowl that the person is there for the purpose of hunting.
17 "§ 113-300.19. Aiding unlicensed persons unlawful.
18 It shall be unlawful for a licensed guide or other resident of North Carolina or any other
19 person to accompany or aid in hunting, in any of the methods described in this Article, any person
20 who has failed to obtain the proper license.
21 "§ 113-300.20. Floating devices restricted.
22 No type of floating device shall be used in the hunting of wild fowl in Currituck Sound except
23 those authorized by the North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission and the Game
24 Commission.
25 "§ 113-300.21. Decoy requirement; use of licensed blinds; temporary locations.
26 (a) It shall be unlawful for any person to shoot waterfowl on the public waters of
27 Currituck County unless the person is lawfully hunting and has a stand of artificial decoys tied
28 out over which the person is shooting.
29 (b) An owner of real estate who has a licensed point blind on the owner's real estate, or
30 the guests of such an owner, may hunt at one unlicensed temporary location per licensed blind
31 on the real estate, provided that the licensed location is not being hunted at the same time and
32 that the temporary location is not within 500 yards of a licensed location belonging to someone
33 other than the owner of the real estate.
34 (c) It shall be unlawful for any person to use a licensed or temporary blind for hunting
35 purposes unless the person (i) has written permission from the licensee of the blind, (ii) has proof
36 of permission in a manner prescribed by the Game Commission, or (iii) is hunting in the presence
37 of the licensee.
38 "§ 113-300.22. Revocation of licenses; criminal penalty.
39 (a) The Game Commission may prosecute and revoke the hunting blind license of any
40 person who has in its judgment violated any part of this Article, or any of such rules and
41 regulations as it may establish, but prior to such revocation, it shall notify the person charged
42 with the violation to appear before the Game Commission on a given day at a given hour. The
43 Game Commission may revoke the hunting blind license of any person who violates any of the
44 provisions of this Article regulating hunting, or who, while hunting, shall go upon the marshes
45 or lands of any person, firm, or corporation without the permission of the owners.
46 (b) Any violation of this Article is a Class 2 misdemeanor, and each violation shall
47 constitute a separate offense.

49 The use of airboats or seaplanes on the public waters of Currituck County is prohibited from
50 September 1 of each year to April 1 of the following year. All boats powered with gasoline, oil,

21 "§ 113-300.25. Vacant blinds.
22 The Game Commission is authorized to close down vacant blinds in its discretion.
23 "§ 113-300.26. Location of blinds; proximity to residences.
24 Every blind of any kind shall be located not less than 500 yards from any blind of any kind.
25 No float blinds shall be tied within 300 yards of any residence. This section shall not apply to
26 blinds closer together than 500 yards on June 12, 1957, but shall be applicable to any such blinds
27 as, and when, one of said blinds becomes vacant.
28 "§ 113-300.27. Renting of blinds prohibited.
29 (a) Any person having a licensed blind is prohibited from renting it by the year or
30 otherwise. The right of a licensee to use a blind is personal to the licensee, and the licensee shall
31 not permit any other person to use the blind except members of the licensee's family, friends, or
32 licensed guides. A licensee shall not accept any money or other thing of value as consideration
33 for the use of the blind, and the licensee shall file a written report each week as to who has been
34 using the blind on request of the Game Commission.
35 (b) The Game Commission is empowered to make reasonable rules and regulations to
36 prevent the renting of blinds. The Game Commission is authorized to revoke the license of any
37 licensee found to have rented the licensee's blind.
38 "§ 113-300.28. Denial of licenses to consistent violators.
39 When the Game Commission finds that an applicant for a blind license is a consistent violator
40 of the game laws of Currituck County, as shown by convictions for such violations, it may in its
41 discretion refuse to issue such applicant a license for any type of blind.

6 (1) No license for a hunting device of any kind, including, but not limited to,
7 stationary stuck or bush blinds and point blinds, shall be granted for any
8 location within 500 yards of any other licensed stationary stuck or bush blind
9 or licensed point.
10 (2) No person shall be granted a license for more than a total of two blinds of any
11 and all types, except property owners, who may have licensed points on their
12 own property without limit so long as the same are not within 500 yards of
13 any other licensed point or stationary bush or stuck blind.
14 (3) Licenses shall be issued only to persons thoroughly familiar with the waters
15 of Currituck Sound, who are capable boat operators, of good moral character,
16 licensed hunters, and aged at least 18 years. In determining good moral
17 character, such things as past record of conviction for violation of the game
18 laws of the State of North Carolina or of the United States, or the violation of
19 valid rules and regulations of the Game Commission of Currituck County or
20 of the Wildlife Resources Commission, shall be considered along with other
21 factors determinative of good moral character.
22 (4) No license shall be assigned or conveyed by the licensee thereof; provided,
23 however, if the licensee dies during the term of the license, the licensee's heirs
24 at law or devisees shall be permitted to operate under said license for the then
25 current hunting season, and no longer, provided that the qualifications
26 specified in this section are met by such successor.
27 "§ 113-300.36. Unlicensed blinds; unauthorized use; removal.
28 (a) No person shall cause to be erected or erect a blind unless the same has been duly
29 licensed as provided in this Article.
30 (b) No person shall shoot or permit another to shoot from a blind which has not been
31 licensed according to the terms of this Article. All licensed blinds shall carry the number of said
32 license on the inside thereof, plainly visible to anyone visiting the blind.
33 (c) No person shall hunt from a licensed blind unless such person shall be the guest of
34 and shall have the permission of the licensee thereof or is in company with such licensee as guide.
35 (d) Any blind or hunting device that is not licensed for a given year shall be immediately
36 demolished by the owner or past owner thereof. If the blind or hunting device is not demolished
37 by August 1 of the current year, law enforcement officers of the Wildlife Resources Commission
38 may enter upon the same and cause the same to be demolished.
39 "§ 113-300.37. Overnight anchoring of float blinds.
40 No float blinds shall be anchored overnight except at a licensed stationary bush or stuck blind
41 or licensed point issued to the licensee of said float blind. All other float blinds must be returned
42 to the landing of the licensee each night.
43 "§ 113-300.38. Rules of the Wildlife Resources Commission.
44 The North Carolina Wildlife Resources Commission shall make such other rules and
45 regulations relative to hunting wild fowl in Currituck Sound as it may deem necessary and proper,
46 not inconsistent with the provisions of this Article."
