1 A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
2 AN ACT TO PROHIBIT THE CITY OF ROCKY MOUNT FROM EXERCISING
3 EXTRATERRITORIAL JURISDICTION OVER CERTAIN DESCRIBED PROPERTIES.
4 The General Assembly of North Carolina enacts:
5 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding G.S. 160D-202 or any other provision of law, the City
6 of Rocky Mount shall not exercise any powers of extraterritorial jurisdiction as provided in
7 Article 2 of Chapter 160D of the General Statutes or its predecessor, Article 19 of Chapter 160A
8 of the General Statutes, over the territory identified by the following Nash County Parcel
9 Identification Numbers: 385214338129U, 385214430568U, and 385214324427U.
10 SECTION 2. The relinquishment of jurisdiction by the City of Rocky Mount over
11 the area identified in Section 1 of this act shall become effective on the date this act becomes
12 law. Notwithstanding G.S. 160D-202(h), the property described in Section 1 of this act is subject
13 to the planning and development regulation jurisdiction, including the zoning authority, of Nash
14 County.
15 SECTION 3. This act is effective when it becomes law.
