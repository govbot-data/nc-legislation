28 SECTION 6.1. If any provision of this act or its application to any person, group of
29 persons, or circumstances is held invalid, the invalidity does not affect other provisions or
30 applications of this act that can be given effect without the invalid provisions or application and,
31 to this end, the provisions of this act are severable.
32 SECTION 6.2. Except as otherwise provided, this act is effective when it becomes
33 law.
